Good to know

Open play questions.

Is open play free?

Open play is free or a small fee. Ask at the counter for today's details.

I've never played a modern board game. Where do I start?

Thursday evenings are Learn to Play nights, 6 to 7 pm, where a different game is taught each week. Special nights like Halloween Werewolf are beginner-friendly too. Or try the game finder.

Is there food?

Yes. There's a snack bar and cooler with sodas, energy drinks and snacks, for purchase.

Can kids play?

Monday's Kids Chess hour runs 5 to 6 pm. Ask us about ages for other events.

Do I need to reserve a table?

Open tables are come-and-play: grab one and start. Want a guaranteed space for your group? Book a private room.
