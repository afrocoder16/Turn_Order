The shop

Take the fun home.

Turn Order stocks modern board games, card games, tabletop RPGs and gaming accessories, from first-game-night picks to things for the long-time hobbyist.

Seasonal pick (October 2026): A spooky Seasonal Selection is stocked for October, with picks like Horrified and haunted-house games.

Shopping is in store. There's no online checkout in this concept, and stock changes, so call (507) 401-3888 to ask whether something's on the shelf.

Good to know

Open play questions.

Is open play free?

Open tables are free or carry a small fee. Confirm with store

Trying a game from the premium library, which has 100+ titles, was planned at $5 to $10 when the shop announced its opening in August 2026. Confirm with store

I've never played a modern board game. Where do I start?

Thursday evenings are Learn to Play nights, 6 to 7 pm, where a different game is taught each week. The Halloween Werewolf night on October 24 is also beginner-friendly. Or try the game finder.

Is there food?

Yes. There's a snack bar and cooler with sodas, energy drinks and snacks, for purchase.

Can kids play?

Monday's Kids Chess hour runs 5 to 6 pm. For other events, ask us about age suitability. Confirm with store

Do I need to reserve a table?

Reservations for open tables aren't posted. Call (507) 401-3888 to ask. If you want a guaranteed space for a group, look at the private rooms. Confirm with store
