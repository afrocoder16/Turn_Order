Why Turn Order

A place to find your people.

Owners Ana and Marcos Marrero moved to Marshall from Florida about four years ago. Marcos hadn't found a regular gaming group, so they opened a place where local players can find each other.

Whether you've played for years or have never rolled a die, there's a table with room for you.

Source: Marshall Independent, Aug. 28, 2026.
