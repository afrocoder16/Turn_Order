Why Turn Order

A place to find your people.

Owners Ana and Marcos Marrero moved to Marshall from Florida about four years ago. Marcos hadn't found a regular gaming group, so they opened a place where local players can find each other.

Marcos describes Turn Order as a clean, comfortable space built by someone who actually plays the games, not a corporate chain. Whether you've played for years or have never rolled a die, there's a table with room for you.

Open since September 2, 2026. Sources: Marshall Independent, Aug. 28, 2026; Turn Order on Facebook.
